# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

---

## Overview

MyTxGNN ("we", "us", "our") operates the MyTxGNN drug repurposing prediction platform (https://mytxgnn.yao.care). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our Service.

---

## Information We Collect

### Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

- **Log Data**: Browser type, pages visited, time spent, referring URL
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system
- **IP Address**: For security and analytics purposes

### Information You Provide

We do not require user registration. If you contact us:

- **Email Communications**: Your email address and message content
- **GitHub Interactions**: Public information from GitHub if you open issues

---

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

1. **Improve the Service**: Analyze usage patterns to enhance functionality
2. **Security**: Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
3. **Communications**: Respond to inquiries when you contact us
4. **Analytics**: Understand how users interact with our platform

---

## Data We Do Not Collect

MyTxGNN does **not** collect:

- Personal health information
- Patient data
- Medical records
- Financial information
- Personal identification documents

---

## Third-Party Services

Our Service may contain links to third-party websites:

- **Harvard TxGNN**: Model and knowledge graph source
- **ClinicalTrials.gov**: Clinical trial data
- **PubMed**: Literature references
- **DrugBank**: Drug information
- **NPRA/data.gov.my**: Malaysia regulatory data
- **GitHub**: Issue tracking and discussions

These sites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

### Resources loaded by this site

The pages load the following third-party resources, which may log access according to their own policies:

| Service | Purpose | Privacy policy |
|---------|---------|----------------|
| GitHub Pages | Site hosting | [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement) |
| Google Analytics 4 (www.googletagmanager.com, google-analytics.com) | Visit statistics (pages viewed, referrer); uses analytics cookies. Search terms, form contents and URL query strings are not sent. | [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| cdn.jsdelivr.net | Script libraries: d3 charts on every page; Fuse.js name matching and the SMART on FHIR client on the SMART pages | [jsDelivr Privacy Policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net) |
| clinicaltrials.gov | Clinical-trial lookup on the SMART pages: only when you ask for trials, the drug and indication names are sent to the ClinicalTrials.gov API | [NLM Web Policies](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/web_policies.html) |
| rxnav.nlm.nih.gov | SMART app: the RxNorm codes of the medications read from your EHR are sent to the NLM RxNav API to look up ingredient names (no patient identifiers) | [NLM Web Policies](https://www.nlm.nih.gov/web_policies.html) |

This site uses Google Analytics 4 analytics cookies (such as `_ga`) to understand site usage. The cookies are set when a page loads. The site sets no cookies of its own for functionality or preferences.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

## Malaysia-Specific Provisions

### Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA)

This Service complies with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010:

- Apart from the Google Analytics 4 usage statistics described above (analytics cookies set when a page loads), the Service does not process personal data
- Usage statistics are processed by Google as our analytics provider; we do not otherwise share personal data with third parties
- Data subjects may request access to their data
