## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available from the structured DrugBank field for this evidence pack (data gap DG002). However, the literature evidence collected here consistently identifies lemborexant as an orally administered **dual orexin receptor antagonist (DORA)**, competitively blocking OX1R and OX2R (with greater affinity for OX2R) to suppress wake-promoting orexin signalling (PMID 32096020, 37086045, 32901578).

Notably, the TxGNN-predicted indication — insomnia (difficulty initiating and/or maintaining sleep) — is the same condition for which lemborexant is already approved in the United States, Japan, and Canada (PMID 32096020, 33636648). This suggests the model has correctly recovered a known, mechanistically well-supported drug–disease relationship rather than surfacing a novel repurposing hypothesis. The value of this evidence pack in this case is less about discovering a new use and more about confirming the strength of existing evidence to support formal indication documentation and market positioning in Malaysia.

Mechanistically, blocking orexin signalling directly addresses the hyperarousal state implicated in insomnia pathophysiology, which is consistent with the consolidated efficacy shown across the SUNRISE 1 and SUNRISE 2 Phase 3 programs and multiple network meta-analyses comparing lemborexant with other DORAs and Z-drugs.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Hold**

**Rationale:**
- The predicted indication is backed by strong, mature evidence (L1: three completed Phase 3 RCTs plus multiple network meta-analyses), and lemborexant is already approved for insomnia in several major markets. However, TFDA/NPRA label data (warnings, contraindications, DDI) is a **Blocking** data gap (DG001) that prevents the required S1 safety pre-assessment, so this candidate cannot advance past evidence review until that gap is closed.
