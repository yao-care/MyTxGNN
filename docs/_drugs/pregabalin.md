## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Detailed mechanism-of-action data is not populated in the drug record for this candidate (`original_moa` is unavailable). However, the evidence pack's repurposing rationale supplies the relevant pharmacology: pregabalin binds the α2δ subunit of voltage-gated calcium channels, reducing presynaptic release of excitatory neurotransmitters (glutamate, norepinephrine, substance P). This action dampens central sensitization — a pain-processing abnormality common to both pregabalin's established neuropathic-pain indications and fibromyalgia.

Multiple trial descriptions within this evidence pack independently confirm that pregabalin is already an established therapy for neuropathic pain conditions (diabetic peripheral neuropathy, postherpetic neuralgia) and as adjunctive treatment for partial seizures, approved in over 130 countries. Fibromyalgia shares the same underlying central-sensitization mechanism as these established indications, which is the pharmacological basis for extending pregabalin's use.

Importantly, the repurposing rationale for fibromyalgia explicitly notes this is an **FDA-approved indication since 2007**, not a purely speculative prediction — pregabalin was one of only three FDA-approved fibromyalgia drugs (alongside duloxetine and milnacipran) as of the most recent literature in this pack (PMID 38279728, 2024). The "prediction" in this Malaysia-market context is therefore best understood as validating whether this internationally established use is reflected in local registration and evidence — which is why the recommendation is "Proceed with Guardrails" rather than a straightforward "Go."
