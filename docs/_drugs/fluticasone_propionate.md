## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available (data gap). Based on known pharmacology, fluticasone propionate is a potent glucocorticoid receptor agonist with anti-inflammatory and anti-pruritic activity, used topically and by inhalation across dermatological and respiratory conditions.

Neurodermatitis (lichen simplex chronicus) is a chronic, itch-scratch cycle-driven lichenified dermatosis. Topical corticosteroids are a commonly used first-line treatment category for this condition in clinical practice, which gives the prediction a plausible class-level mechanistic basis.

However, this evidence pack contains no drug-specific comparative or observational study of fluticasone propionate in neurodermatitis — the two available publications are a general review of lichen simplex and an unrelated case report. The link is therefore a pharmacological-class extrapolation rather than direct evidence.
