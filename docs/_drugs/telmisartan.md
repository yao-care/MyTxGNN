## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, a formal mechanism-of-action (MOA) record is not available in this evidence pack (flagged as data gap DG002). Based on the repurposing rationale provided alongside the prediction, telmisartan is an angiotensin II type 1 (AT1) receptor blocker that also carries partial PPAR-γ agonist activity. Beyond its blood-pressure-lowering effect, preclinical studies cited in the evidence pack show telmisartan reduces neuroinflammation, oxidative stress, and neurovascular unit injury in animal stroke models — a plausible mechanistic basis for a role in secondary stroke prevention.

Hypertension is the single largest modifiable risk factor for stroke, so a well-established antihypertensive with additional neuroprotective/anti-inflammatory properties is a mechanistically reasonable repurposing candidate. This is reflected in the fact that several major outcome trials (ONTARGET, TRANSCEND, PRoFESS) were explicitly designed with stroke or composite cardio-cerebrovascular events as endpoints.

That said, the evidence pack's own rationale flags an important caveat: these key large-scale RCTs generally showed **neutral** results — telmisartan was found *non-inferior* to comparators (ramipril, placebo-on-background-therapy) rather than *superior* for stroke-specific outcomes. The mechanistic and epidemiological signal is real, but it has not translated into a proven independent stroke-prevention benefit beyond what would be expected from blood pressure control alone. Evidence strength (trial volume/phase) and demonstrated clinical benefit for stroke as a distinct new indication should therefore be assessed separately.

## Conclusion and Next Steps

**Decision: Proceed with Guardrails**

**Rationale:**
Stroke is the highest-ranked TxGNN prediction and is backed by a large volume of trial and mechanistic evidence, including trials (ONTARGET, PRoFESS, TRIDENT) whose primary endpoints directly involve stroke. However, the pivotal trials showed largely *neutral/non-inferior* — not superior — results specifically for stroke outcomes, so evidence level L1 reflects trial volume/phase rather than a proven independent stroke-prevention benefit distinct from blood-pressure control.

**To proceed, the following is needed:**
- NPRA/package insert safety data (warnings, contraindications, DDI) — currently a Blocking gap (DG001)
- Formal mechanism-of-action documentation from DrugBank or equivalent (DG002)
- A focused re-analysis or meta-analysis isolating stroke-specific (not composite CV) outcomes from ONTARGET/TRANSCEND/PRoFESS to clarify true incremental benefit
- Clarification of the duplicate/near-duplicate disease terms in the prediction set (hypertension vs. hypertensive disorder; stroke disorder vs. cerebrovascular disorder vs. the obsolete "susceptibility to ischemic stroke" term) before this candidate advances further
