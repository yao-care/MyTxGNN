## Why is This Prediction Reasonable?

Currently, detailed mechanism of action data is not available (flagged as a High-severity data gap, DG002). Based on known pharmacology, Procaine benzylpenicillin is a narrow-spectrum bactericidal penicillin that inhibits bacterial transpeptidase (PBP), blocking cell wall synthesis in penicillin-susceptible organisms.

The mechanistic rationale for infectious otitis media is plausible by analogy to suppurative otitis media, since many classic otitis media pathogens (e.g., *S. pneumoniae*, *S. pyogenes*) are penicillin-susceptible. However, unlike the related candidate "suppurative otitis media" in this evidence pack (which has historical clinical trial literature from the 1950s), the "infectious otitis media" label specifically has **no clinical trials or literature identified** in any of the queried sources (ClinicalTrials.gov, ICTRP, PubMed all returned 0 results). This prediction should therefore be treated as a hypothesis generated purely by the TxGNN model score, not as evidence-supported repurposing.
